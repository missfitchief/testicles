# Testicles visual system

The established identity is preserved: pale mint surfaces, evergreen type, Albert Sans and the original glossy mascot. The opening keeps its oversized lowercase title and HYPE coin composition.

The story has three full-width cinematic chapters after the opening: the slide and group landing, the leap into an adult man's trousers, and the exit into conversation with a woman. Each has its own ordinary native film and a matching idle loop. Captions change at actual character-action beats. The final community button closes the page; there is no separate footer or contract panel.

Scrolling moves the page and small transform/opacity transitions. It does not control video time. Browser-native muted playback owns all character motion; only the current chapter plays. Still images cover media initialization and failures. The implementation has no custom frame decoding, worker, canvas, scroll hijacking or variable video speed.

Desktop films are 2560 × 1440; portrait-phone films are 2048 × 2048, cropped in the layout to remove the source's letterbox padding. All are the source's original 24fps, with high-quality H.264 encoding. Still images retain their full 2K-or-larger dimensions. Soft edges merge phone scenes with the page surface, while all copy and touch targets remain outside the art. Tablet portrait layouts show the whole wide composition above the text.

Reduced-motion visitors keep the automatic character films with interface travel removed. Hover applies only to fine pointers. Keyboard focus, contract-copy feedback and external-link behavior are preserved.
